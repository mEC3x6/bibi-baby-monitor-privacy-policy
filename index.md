## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Baby Monitor mobile application ("the App") handles information and uses device permissions.

Baby Monitor is designed to help parents and caregivers monitor sound levels in a room and automatically initiate a cellular telephone call to a user-configured phone number when the selected noise threshold and detection conditions are met.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the information processed by the App.

## 2. Information Processed by the App

### Microphone and audio information

The App requires access to your device's microphone to measure sound levels and detect noise.

Microphone audio is processed locally on the device in real time to calculate an approximate sound level and determine whether the configured noise threshold has been exceeded.

The App does not intentionally record, save, upload, or transmit microphone audio to the developer or to an application-operated server.

The App is designed for sound detection, not audio recording or remote audio streaming.

### Telephone number

You may enter a telephone number that the App will call when its noise detection conditions are satisfied.

The number is stored locally on your device using the App's local storage.

The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores your configured telephone number.

When a call is initiated, the number is passed to the Android telephone system to place a normal cellular call. Your mobile network operator processes the call under its own services and privacy policies.

### App settings

The App stores settings locally, which may include:

- The configured telephone number.
- Noise detection sensitivity or threshold.
- Noise confirmation and reset timings.
- Startup delay and other monitoring preferences.

These settings are used to provide the App's functionality.

## 3. Device Permissions

Baby Monitor uses the following Android permissions:

**Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)**

Required to measure sound levels and detect noise.

**Phone calls (`CALL_PHONE`)**

Required to initiate telephone calls automatically when the monitoring conditions are met, or when you use the test-call functionality.

**Modify audio settings (`MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS`)**

Supports audio functionality and microphone access within the Android application.

**Internet (`INTERNET`)**

Included in the Android application configuration as part of its application framework. The App's core monitoring and cellular calling features are designed to function without an internet connection.

The App does not intentionally send microphone audio or saved monitoring settings to a developer-operated online service.

You can manage applicable permissions through your device's Android Settings. Denying microphone or calling permissions may prevent the corresponding features from working.

## 4. Phone Calls and Mobile Network Operators

The App may automatically initiate a standard cellular call to your configured number after the noise threshold and confirmation conditions are satisfied.

These calls use the telephone functionality of your device and mobile network.

Your mobile operator may process telephone numbers, call records, connection information and billing information according to its own privacy policy.

Normal mobile calling charges may apply.

The developer does not control your mobile operator's collection or retention of telephone records.

## 5. Data Storage and Backups

The App stores its configuration locally on your device.

The Android application is configured to disable standard Android cloud backup and exclude application data from supported Android backup and device-to-device transfer mechanisms.

However, some device manufacturers may provide independent transfer or backup mechanisms that operate outside the App's control.

The developer does not provide a cloud backup or account synchronization service.

## 6. Analytics, Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate advertising networks, behavioral tracking services, or developer-operated analytics services.

The App does not require a user account or login.

It does use Android, WebView, Capacitor, and other software components needed to operate. Android system components and your mobile network may separately process technical information according to their respective policies.

The developer does not sell personal information.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Baby Monitor is intended for use by parents, guardians and other caregivers, rather than directly by children.

The App may detect sounds made by a baby or another person in the monitored room. Such audio is processed locally for noise detection and is not intentionally stored or uploaded by the App.

The App does not provide child accounts, advertising profiles, or direct messaging features.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Saved telephone numbers and monitoring settings generally remain on your device while the App's local data is retained.

You can remove this information by clearing the App's storage through Android Settings or uninstalling the App, subject to Android's normal device storage and backup behavior.

The developer does not maintain a separate account database containing this information and therefore does not provide server-side account deletion.

## 9. Security

We aim to minimize privacy risks by processing microphone audio locally, limiting stored information to application settings, and avoiding unnecessary online accounts or developer-operated data storage.

No device or software system can be guaranteed completely secure.

We recommend keeping your Android device updated and protecting it with an appropriate screen lock.
